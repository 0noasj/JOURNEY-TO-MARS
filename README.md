ABOUT THE GAME


THE STORY
the year is 2420 and earth is dying you play as dogelon mars accompanied by your loyal dog rufus your mission is to pilot the ares vanguard to mars and activate the genesis core along the way you will face the annihilators not alien monsters but corrupted corporate ai from ancient earth

THE OBJECTIVE
your main goal is to carefully manage your resources survive the dangerous space journey overcome obstacles and finally defeat the mothership to bring the golden age back to mars

STAGES AND STRUCTURE
the game features around 35 interconnected story slides with an irregular branching structure to keep your mind engaged and prevent the gameplay from feeling repetitive

KEY FEATURES
a dynamic non linear narrative where every choice matters a smart button lock system to protect your assets a cool blend of crypto concepts with sci fi storytelling and a live ui tracking your status

GAME RESOURCES
fuel for physical maneuvers energy for shields and tech health representing hull integrity and elon tokens your digital wealth used to unlock ancestral paths

CORE MECHANICS
instant death checks mean if physical resources hit zero the game ends immediately giving you a narrative reason for the failure the token burn mechanic turns a financial concept into a cool way to solve crises plus a soft lock system prevents your balance from ever going negative

CHARACTERS
dogelon mars the brave protagonist rufus your faithful companion the methuselah foundation your deep space allies and the annihilators the robotic antagonists

ATMOSPHERE
the vibe is a mix of cosmic isolation the fear of navigating hazards and an epic hopeful atmosphere once you finally reach the red planet

DIFFICULTY AND PLAYTIME
set to medium it is challenging enough for hardcore gamers but fair enough for casual players each run takes about 5 to 10 minutes but high replay value comes from multiple paths and different endings

THE ENDINGS
there are three completely different epic endings including a heroic sacrifice reaching a golden paradise and becoming the spirit of the planet itself along with several failure states based on your mistakes
PLATFORM AND ENGINE
built on the powerful twine engine using the harlowe 3 format customized with html and css for a beautiful ui and fully playable in any web browser

WHY CHOOSE THIS GAME
this is not just a simple text adventure it is a highly engineered bug free experience i turned dry concepts like token burning into thrilling story mechanics the logic is air tight preventing bugs like negative resources by giving clear reasons for every failure and delivering shocking endings this game leaves a lasting impression on players and judges alike
